## [0.1.0] - unreleased

### Added

- Magic Home devices found on the local network, without any account: a UDP search on port 48899 through the network discovery of Gladys, plus IP addresses typed in the configuration for the devices the search cannot reach; each device is identified by its MAC address, found again after an address change, and remembered in `/data` so a restart reconnects at once.
- Lights in the Discover tab with the features their model and wiring offer: on/off, brightness, colour and colour temperature; bulbs, LED strip controllers (RGB, RGBW, RGBWW, CCT, single colour), CCT bulbs, addressable strips and Christmas lights; sockets and switches as on/off switches.
- Every generation of the protocol: the original one, the 8- and 9-byte checksum protocols, the 25-byte HSV firmware of the newest bulbs and the wrapped frames of the addressable strips and table lamps.
- States read every 10 s to 1 min over a single connection per device, and the changes the devices report on their own (remote control, Magic Home app) published at once, within the Gladys rate limit, with a local or unreachable badge per device.
- Dashboard widgets "Magic Home lights" (every device: on, off or not answering, brightness, effect, "All off" button) and "Magic Home effects" (one light: state, brightness, on/off and three effect buttons chosen in the settings).
- Scene triggers: a device stops answering, a device answers again.
- Scene actions: start a built-in effect (speed, brightness), play up to 16 colours of your choice, flash a colour then come back to the previous state, stop an effect.
- Configuration button "Search and test the devices".
- Texts in English, French, German and Spanish.
